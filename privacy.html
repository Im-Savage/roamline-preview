Roamline · Map providers and privacy

Location and offline walks

Location permission lets Roamline show your position and estimated accuracy. Offline route planning, saved preferences and the last outward walk stay on your device. Browsing location stops when the map is no longer visible. Optional pocket mode can keep guidance active with the screen locked during a walk.

Google Maps

Choosing Google Maps loads the provider’s online map and sends network requests for the area you view. Following your position centers the map on it. Looking up a place sends the selected name and search area. Opening walking directions sends endpoints and selected stops. Scenic walking routes are calculated by Roamline using OpenStreetMap.

Use of the Google Maps features is subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Maps Demo credentials are for evaluation only.

Tripadvisor

The starter integration lets you copy a place name and open Tripadvisor. When licensed Terra access is configured, requesting a listing or reviews sends the place search or listing ID via the provider server. These requests exclude your live GPS and walking history. Roamline does not keep reviews or ratings in its offline pack. See Tripadvisor’s terms and privacy policy.
